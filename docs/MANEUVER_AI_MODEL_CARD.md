# ORION Maneuver AI Model Card

## Purpose

`ORION simulated two-body maneuver miss-distance ranker` (v1.0.0) predicts the bounded existing two-body simulator's closest-approach distance for each generated nonzero burn candidate. Scores are diagnostic and do not change candidate order: held-out performance did not outperform the minimum-delta-v baseline. This is a surrogate of simulator output; it does not estimate collision probability, safety, or actual maneuver effectiveness and does not discover candidates outside the existing sweep.

## Training data and target

There was no local historical dataset containing validated burn actions and outcomes. The model was trained on deterministic synthetic Cartesian scenarios generated by `backend/app/ai/maneuver_training.py`. Each target is the `predicted_miss_distance_km` returned by the existing `optimize_maneuver` routine. Zero-burn records are excluded from model training, and candidates whose simulator minimum lies on the search boundary are excluded from training and evaluation.

The 60 independent scenarios were split 60/20/20 by scenario ID with seed 1501: 36 train, 12 validation, 12 test. All candidates from one scenario remain in one partition. The validation set was reserved and not used to tune the fixed estimator configuration. Test results cover 12 independent scenarios and 72 candidate rows.

The test MAE was 4.898 km, RMSE 7.185 km, R² -0.274, and candidate-level Spearman correlation 0.389. Within-scenario mean Spearman was -0.069. The model's first-ranked candidate matched the simulator-best candidate in 3/12 scenarios (25%) and had 0.059 km mean miss-distance regret. A minimum-Δv baseline had 0.037 km mean regret. The learned ranking therefore did not outperform this simple baseline in this small synthetic test.

## Features

The 20 model inputs contain primary TCA position and velocity (TEME, km and km/s), relative secondary-minus-primary TCA position and velocity in TEME (km and km/s), burn magnitude (m/s), lead time from burn to TCA (seconds), and six one-hot RTN burn-direction labels. State vectors must be finite, in TEME, at the same UTC instant as TCA, and associated with the selected event's primary/secondary IDs. Unsupported or missing inputs make ranking unavailable.

The estimator is `RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=160, max_depth=12, min_samples_leaf=2, max_features=1.0, random_state=1501, n_jobs=1)`. The serialized artifact and schema metadata are in `backend/app/ai/artifacts/`.

## Use and limitations

The API loads the hash/version/schema-checked artifact and scores the actual candidates from the job-bound selected event. The two-body simulator still independently evaluates each candidate; ML scores cannot change propagation, physical outcomes, baseline comparisons, or candidate feasibility. Search-boundary candidates do not receive an ML rank. The frontend and PDF identify model/version, score meaning, event/job provenance, and research limitations.

All records and labels are synthetic simulator outputs. The model has not been validated against real maneuver outcomes. The simulator itself omits covariance, Pc, execution error, attitude, propulsion, J2, drag, and third-body effects. A lower simulator miss-distance score is not proof of a safe or effective maneuver, and the current test did not show improved ranking over the minimum-Δv baseline. Browser-level interaction remains unverified.

To reproduce dataset generation and training offline from `backend`, run `.venv\\Scripts\\python.exe -m app.ai.maneuver_training`. This command overwrites the v1 synthetic dataset and model artifact; runtime inference does not train.
